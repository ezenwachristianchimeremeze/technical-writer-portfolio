My journey

I am Ezenwa Christian Chimeremeze, a technology professional from Imo State, Nigeria. My current professional focus is technical writing and developer documentation: making software systems, APIs, tools, and engineering concepts easier for people to understand and use.

I grew up in a poor background in Obinwanne, Umuaka, with family roots in Ekwe, Ebenano, Isu Local Government Area, Imo State. My upbringing shaped my determination to improve my family's circumstances, make my parents proud, and contribute to the community that raised me. It also strengthened my belief in perseverance, empathy, and sharing opportunities.

I completed my secondary education at Ekwe Secondary School in 2026. I began studying programming seriously in 2023, starting with Python and later exploring web development, APIs, databases, automation, and artificial intelligence.

I have continued learning and building with limited resources, including using an Android phone as a development environment. This experience has influenced my interest in practical learning, accessible technology, and clear technical communication.

Technical writing and professional interests

I focus on documentation and articles that help developers understand and work with technology. My subject areas include Python, FastAPI, API design and rate limiting, Docker, Redis, backend security, AI agents, and software development workflows.

My technical writing has appeared on Dev.to, SitePoint, and Medium. My public profiles provide the best current record of my published work.
