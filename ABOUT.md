## My journey

I am Ezenwa Christian Chimeremeze, a technology professional from Imo State, Nigeria, building a career around technical writing, developer documentation, and clear communication about software and emerging technology.

I grew up in a poor background in Obinwanne, Umuaka, with family roots in Ekwe, Ebenano, Isu Local Government Area, Imo State. Those experiences shaped my determination to create better opportunities for my family and contribute meaningfully to the community I come from. They also taught me the importance of perseverance, empathy, and sharing opportunities with others.

I completed my secondary education at Ekwe Secondary School in 2026. I began studying programming seriously in 2023, initially focusing on Python and expanding into web development, APIs, databases, automation, and artificial intelligence.

My route into technology has involved learning and building with limited equipment and resources, including working from an Android phone. That experience strengthened my interest in practical, accessible technical education and in explaining complex ideas clearly.
